# WSQS International Toolkit v0.9.0

First version prepared for public distribution from the frozen technical
baseline 0.9.0-rc4. The package includes the international CORE-8 and full
research surveys, Word/PDF masters, a blank nine-sheet Excel calculator,
Python scoring and descriptive workflows, optional extension support and
researcher documentation. Synthetic demonstration files are explicitly labeled.

## Scientific scope

Original WSQS CORE-8 scoring and original predictor/model configurations are
unchanged. International forecasting requires local harmonization of the
33 original predictors and separate study-specific training and validation.
The adapted survey wording is not independently validated for all populations.

## Data privacy

No observed household-level Tamil Nadu data or private QA records are
included. The 25 public example records are synthetic. The original-study
reproducibility archive is separate and is not covered by this release.
