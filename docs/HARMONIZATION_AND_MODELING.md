# HARMONIZATION AND MODELING


Original CORE-8 scores are computed directly.

Forecasting requires 22 service-support, three context and eight priority predictors, totaling 33.

Record local response mappings, units, currencies, provider eligibility, missingness and validation evidence in the harmonization template.

Never silently equate original India-specific encodings with an international adaptation.

Train and validate models on new-study data separately.

Predictive findings are not causal effects.
